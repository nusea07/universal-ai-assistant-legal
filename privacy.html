Privacy Policy

Application: UNIVERSAL AI ASSISTANT

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

UNIVERSAL AI ASSISTANT is an AI-powered Instagram messaging platform developed to help participating businesses automate customer communication and respond to inquiries.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and protected when businesses connect their Instagram accounts to our application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, we may process:

  • Instagram account identifiers and usernames.
  • Messages sent to participating Instagram businesses.
  • Message content, timestamps, and conversation context.
  • Images and other media shared in supported conversations.
  • Comments and story interactions when supported.
  • Business account information necessary for integration.
  • Instagram authorization tokens and technical data.

We do not require businesses to provide their Instagram passwords directly to our application. Authentication is performed through Meta's authorization mechanisms.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Provide automated responses to customer inquiries.
  • Understand customer questions and conversation context.
  • Retrieve relevant business product or service information.
  • Support customer service and human assistance.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the integration.
  • Protect the application against unauthorized access.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

The application may use third-party AI services, including OpenAI, to interpret messages and generate relevant responses.

Information necessary to process a request may be transmitted securely to these service providers.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application uses cloud infrastructure and databases to support Instagram integrations.

Instagram authorization tokens are stored in encrypted form where supported by the application's token storage system.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect processed information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database infrastructure, and AI processing providers.

Relevant information may also be made available to the participating business for customer service purposes.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, fulfill legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable law.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, the connected business, and applicable requirements.
